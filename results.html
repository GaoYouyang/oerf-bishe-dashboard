1. 可信的物理仿真与评测平台1. A trustworthy simulation and evaluation platform

实现了多相机 BOS 前向模型、精确伴随、CGLS/PCGLS、Gaussian 条件均值、四项误差指标和算子调用计数。关键算子由两套独立代码交叉检验，结果一致到约 1e-12。Built the multi-camera BOS forward model, exact adjoint, CGLS/PCGLS, Gaussian posterior mean, four error metrics and operator-call accounting. Key operators are cross-checked by two independent codes, agreeing to about 1e-12.

范围Scope

2. Gaussian 统计对照是很强的基线2. The Gaussian baseline is very strong

只用训练场估计协方差的 Gaussian 条件均值，在 9/9 条训练轨迹上满足全部精度上限；而从零开始的 CGLS/PCGLS（4–7 步）和反投影一条都不满足。在严格的跨轨迹留出检验中，它通过 7/9 条。The Gaussian posterior mean, with covariance estimated from training fields only, meets every limit on 9/9 training trajectories, while zero-start CGLS/PCGLS (4–7 steps) and back-projection meet none. Under strict held-out-trajectory tests it passes 7/9.

局限Limits
两条留出轨迹的梯度误差仍超限，说明训练分布之外它并非万能。Two held-out trajectories still exceed the gradient limit, so it is not sufficient outside the training distribution.
有收益，有代价gain with trade-offs

3. 粗网格上，学习先验带来真实收益3. On a coarse grid, a learned prior gives real gains

在 41×17×17 网格上，学习得到的条件先验在 1010 个留出帧上全部达到参考精度，场与梯度联合误差比强统计对照低 9.25%。同一个五相机模型不重新训练就能用于 7/9/12 相机、去掉一台相机和 1% 噪声的情况。不经 CGLS 的完整首次运行比统计对照快约 17–18%。On a 41×17×17 grid, a learned conditional prior matched the reference on all 1,010 held-out frames and lowered the joint field/gradient error by 9.25% against the strong statistical baseline. The same five-camera model, without retraining, worked with 7/9/12 cameras, with one camera removed, and with 1% noise. A full first run without CGLS was about 17–18% faster than the statistical baseline.

局限Limits
两条轨迹的观测误差高出约 7%，超出容差；峰值内存高 3–5%；统计对照本身更准。这些收益没有在原生网格上复现。Observation error was ~7% too high on two trajectories; peak memory was 3–5% higher; the statistical baseline itself was more accurate. These gains did not carry over to the native grid.
负面结果negative

4. 原生网格上，学习先验没能超过 Gaussian4. On the native grid, learned priors did not beat the Gaussian

在 200×80×80 网格上试了几十种初值设计（谱方法、协方差压缩、射线图、条件神经网络、时序传输等），在训练轨迹上都达不到 Gaussian 的精度。利用前一帧的时序模型训练不稳定，最终误差约为 Gaussian 的 4.9 倍。以 Gaussian 为底再学一个修正，虽然 909 帧全部匹配，但联合误差只降低约 0.01%。Dozens of initial-guess designs on the 200×80×80 grid (spectral, compressed covariance, ray graphs, conditional networks, temporal transport, …) failed to reach Gaussian accuracy even on the training trajectories. A temporal model reusing the previous frame trained unstably and ended at about 4.9× the Gaussian error. A learned correction on top of the Gaussian matched all 909 frames but lowered the joint error by only ~0.01%.

结论Lesson
在当前测量规模下，Gaussian 已接近可达精度。这些是训练集上的失败，不能归咎于泛化或算力。At the current measurement count the Gaussian is close to the achievable accuracy. These are failures on the training data, so they cannot be blamed on generalisation or compute.

5. 计算成本：经典加速已经很强，学习型初值要在它之上省5. Cost: classical acceleration is strong; a learned start must save on top of it

固定相机时，Gaussian 只需预先分解一个约 1 万 × 1 万的矩阵，之后每帧一次回代，学习方法无从节省。真实 BOS 每台相机约百万像素，测量数在 10⁷ 量级，稠密矩阵需要约 800 TB，只能迭代求解。迭代步数随测量数增长（测量数翻 4 倍，步数约翻 2 倍），1% 噪声下要多约 3 倍，而 Nyström 预条件在那里的加速更大（6–16 倍）。With fixed cameras the Gaussian needs one factorisation of a ~10⁴ × 10⁴ matrix and then one back-substitution per frame, so a learned method has nothing to save. Real BOS cameras have ~10⁶ pixels each, giving ~10⁷ measurements; the dense matrix would need about 800 TB, so the solve must be iterative. The iteration count grows with the measurement count (4× more measurements, about 2× more steps) and roughly triples at 1% noise, where the Nyström preconditioner helps even more (6–16×).

意义Why it matters
“比从零开始快”不再是有意义的标准。学习型初值必须在 Nyström 加前一帧之上继续省步数，而且自身开销要低于省下的步数。“Faster than a zero start” is no longer a meaningful bar. A learned start must save steps on top of Nyström plus the previous frame, and cost less than the steps it saves.
局限Limits
停止点用真值判断，是诊断性测量，不是可部署的停止规则。The stopping point is judged against ground truth, so this is a diagnostic, not a deployable stopping rule.

到目前为止学到的What we have learned

  • 算子调用少，不等于更快：一个经典初值少用了 72.7% 的调用，耗时却和对照几乎相同，内存还大得多。Fewer operator calls do not mean faster: one classical start used 72.7% fewer calls but took the same time and far more memory.
  • 训练损失下降，不等于重建更好：有几个小模型训练损失降了 13–27%，重建却没有改善。Lower training loss does not mean better reconstructions: several small models cut their training loss by 13–27% with no gain in the final result.
  • 先问“价值在哪个区间”，再设计方法：与其在强基线最强的地方硬拼，不如找到它代价最高的地方。Ask where the value lies before designing a method: compete where the strong baseline is expensive, not where it is strongest.
