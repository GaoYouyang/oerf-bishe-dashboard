1. 可信的物理仿真与评测平台1. A trustworthy simulation and evaluation platform

实现了多相机 BOS 前向模型、精确伴随、CGLS/PCGLS、Gaussian 条件均值、四项误差指标和算子调用计数。关键算子由两套独立代码交叉检验，结果一致到约 1e-12。Built the multi-camera BOS forward model, exact adjoint, CGLS/PCGLS, Gaussian posterior mean, four error metrics and operator-call accounting. Key operators are cross-checked by two independent codes, agreeing to about 1e-12.

范围Scope
原生 200×80×80 网格，5/7/9 相机，909 帧。Native 200×80×80 grid, 5/7/9 cameras, 909 frames.
意义Why it matters
后面所有结论都建立在这套可复算的基础上。Every later conclusion rests on this reproducible base.

2. Gaussian 统计对照是很强的基线2. The Gaussian baseline is very strong

只用训练场估计协方差的 Gaussian 条件均值，在 9/9 条训练轨迹上满足全部精度上限；而从零开始的 CGLS/PCGLS（4–7 步）和反投影一条都不满足。在严格的跨轨迹留出检验中，它通过 7/9 条。The Gaussian posterior mean, with covariance estimated from training fields only, meets every limit on 9/9 training trajectories, while zero-start CGLS/PCGLS (4–7 steps) and back-projection meet none. Under strict held-out-trajectory tests it passes 7/9.

局限Limits
两条留出轨迹的梯度误差仍超限，说明训练分布之外它并非万能。Two held-out trajectories still exceed the gradient limit, so it is not sufficient outside the training distribution.
有收益，有代价gain with trade-offs

3. 粗网格上，学习先验带来真实收益3. On a coarse grid, a learned prior gives real gains

在 41×17×17 网格上，学习得到的条件先验在 1010 个留出帧上全部达到参考精度，场与梯度联合误差比强统计对照低 9.25%。同一个五相机模型不重新训练就能用于 7/9/12 相机、去掉一台相机和 1% 噪声的情况。不经 CGLS 的完整首次运行比统计对照快约 17–18%。On a 41×17×17 grid, a learned conditional prior matched the reference on all 1,010 held-out frames and lowered the joint field/gradient error by 9.25% against the strong statistical baseline. The same five-camera model, without retraining, worked with 7/9/12 cameras, with one camera removed, and with 1% noise. A full first run without CGLS was about 17–18% faster than the statistical baseline.

局限Limits
两条轨迹的观测误差高出约 7%，超出容差；峰值内存高 3–5%；统计对照本身更准。这些收益没有在原生网格上复现。Observation error was ~7% too high on two trajectories; peak memory was 3–5% higher; the statistical baseline itself was more accurate. These gains did not carry over to the native grid.
负面结果negative

4. 原生网格上，学习先验没能超过 Gaussian4. On the native grid, learned priors did not beat the Gaussian

在 200×80×80 网格上试了几十种初值设计（谱方法、协方差压缩、射线图、条件神经网络、时序传输等），在训练轨迹上都达不到 Gaussian 的精度。利用前一帧的时序模型训练不稳定，最终误差约为 Gaussian 的 4.9 倍。以 Gaussian 为底再学一个修正，虽然 909 帧全部匹配，但联合误差只降低约 0.01%。Dozens of initial-guess designs on the 200×80×80 grid (spectral, compressed covariance, ray graphs, conditional networks, temporal transport, …) failed to reach Gaussian accuracy even on the training trajectories. A temporal model reusing the previous frame trained unstably and ended at about 4.9× the Gaussian error. A learned correction on top of the Gaussian matched all 909 frames but lowered the joint error by only ~0.01%.

结论Lesson
在当前测量规模下，Gaussian 已接近可达精度。这些是训练集上的失败，不能归咎于泛化或算力。At the current measurement count the Gaussian is close to the achievable accuracy. These are failures on the training data, so they cannot be blamed on generalisation or compute.
初步，进行中preliminary

5. 计算成本：经典加速已经很强，学习型初值要在它之上省5. Cost: classical acceleration is strong; a learned start must save on top of it

固定相机时，Gaussian 只需预先分解一个约 1 万 × 1 万的矩阵，之后每帧一次回代，学习方法无从节省。真实 BOS 每台相机约百万像素，测量数在 10⁷ 量级，稠密矩阵需要约 800 TB，只能迭代求解。迭代步数随测量数增长（测量数翻 4 倍，步数约翻 2 倍），1% 噪声下要多约 3 倍，而 Nyström 预条件在那里的加速也更大（秩 400 和 1600 分别快 6 倍和 16 倍）。With fixed cameras the Gaussian needs one factorisation of a ~10⁴ × 10⁴ matrix and then one back-substitution per frame, so a learned method has nothing to save. Real BOS cameras have ~10⁶ pixels each, giving ~10⁷ measurements; the dense matrix would need about 800 TB, so the solve must be iterative. The iteration count grows with the measurement count (4× more measurements, about 2× more steps) and roughly triples at 1% noise, where the Nyström preconditioner also helps more (6× at rank 400 and 16× at rank 1600).

意义Why it matters
“比从零开始快”不再是有意义的标准。学习型初值必须在 Nyström 加前一帧之上继续省步数，而且自身开销要低于省下的步数；第 6 条表明这个门槛还能更低。“Faster than a zero start” is no longer a meaningful bar. A learned start must save steps on top of Nyström plus the previous frame, and cost less than the steps it saves; item 6 shows the bar can go lower still.
局限Limits
停止点用真值判断，是诊断性测量，不是可部署的停止规则。The stopping point is judged against ground truth, so this is a diagnostic, not a deployable stopping rule.

6. 换一种求解形式，同样精度下步数约减半6. A different formulation halves the steps at equal accuracy

同一个 Gaussian 后验均值也可以在白化原空间里求解：令 ρ = μ + Lw，其中 C = LLᵀ 直接由先验读出（与 C 的差约 1e-16）。两种形式每步的算子开销相同，还能从完全相同的初始场出发。不加预条件时，原空间从零开始只要 128–192 步（对偶形式 384 步），从前一帧开始要 96 步（对偶形式 192–384 步）。加 Nyström 预条件后，原空间秩 100 加前一帧只要 24–32 步；对偶形式秩 400 加前一帧要 32–48 步，准备成本也更高（400 次对 150 次算子调用）。The same Gaussian posterior mean can also be solved in the whitened primal: ρ = μ + Lw, with C = LLᵀ read directly off the prior (matching C to about 1e-16). Both forms cost the same operator calls per step and can start from exactly the same field. Without preconditioning the primal needs 128–192 steps from zero (dual: 384) and 96 from the previous frame (dual: 192–384). With Nyström preconditioning, primal rank 100 plus the previous frame needs 24–32 steps, against 32–48 for dual rank 400 plus the previous frame, which also costs more to set up (400 against 150 operator calls).

剩下的步数主要花在观测残差上，每次运行都是它最后达标：从前一帧出发，场和梯度大约 8–12 步就与 Gaussian 相当，观测残差要 24–32 步。一个用其他轨迹数据学出的线性修正把场误差降了约 30%，步数却没有减少；用前几帧外推或在其张成的子空间里取最优初值，也几乎不省步数。The remaining steps go mostly into the observation residual, the last metric to match in every run: from the previous frame, the field and gradients match the Gaussian after about 8–12 steps, while the observation residual needs 24–32. A linear correction learned from other trajectories cut the field error by about 30% yet saved no steps, and extrapolating from earlier frames or taking the best start in their span saved almost none.

范围Scope
无噪声合成数据，五相机，9690 个测量，原生网格。只用了 9 条轨迹中 3 条的开发帧（求解形式对比 9 帧，初值对比 12 帧），其余 6 条留作确认集，尚未评估。停止点用真值判断（四项指标都在 Gaussian 的 1.01 倍以内），是诊断性测量，不是实际可用的停止规则。Noise-free synthetic data, five cameras, 9,690 measurements, native grid. Development frames from 3 of the 9 trajectories only (9 frames for the formulation comparison, 12 for the starting points); the other 6 are kept as a confirmation set and have not been evaluated yet. Stopping is judged against ground truth (all four metrics within 1.01× of the Gaussian), so these are diagnostics, not a practical stopping rule.
意义Why it matters
经典门槛进一步降低。学习型初值只有降低观测残差才可能省步数；若能把前一帧的误差缩到 1/3，步数可降到 6–16。The classical bar drops again. A learned start can save steps only by lowering the observation residual; shrinking the previous frame's error to a third would bring the count to 6–16.
有条件conditional

7. 真实数据上，预条件的收益取决于先验7. On real data, the preconditioner's gain depends on the prior

在宾州州立大学公开的真实 BOS 层析数据上（1063 万条射线，32³ 网格），以与收敛参考解相差 1% 为判据，不需要三维真值。不加平滑先验时，零初值共轭梯度和秩 32、64 的 Nyström 都要 64 次正反投影，算上准备成本，预条件没有净收益；原因是算子的谱衰减很慢，前 64 个特征值只相差 3.7 倍。加上高斯平滑先验（σ = 1.5 体素）后，前 64 个特征值相差 26 倍，Nyström 秩 64 在 48 次时达标，零初值要 64 次；步数相同时，误差小 4–10 倍。On the public Penn State real BOS tomography data (10.6 million rays, 32³ grid), with a 1% match to a converged reference as the criterion, so no 3D truth is needed. Without a smoothing prior, zero-start conjugate gradients and rank-32 and rank-64 Nyström all need 64 forward–adjoint passes, so the preconditioner gives no net gain once set-up is counted: the spectrum decays slowly, only 3.7× over the first 64 eigenvalues. With a Gaussian smoothness prior (σ = 1.5 voxels) the decay steepens to 26×, rank-64 Nyström meets the criterion at 48 passes against 64 from zero, and at equal step count its error is 4–10× smaller.

局限Limits
准备预条件要 64 次投影，多于单次求解省下的步数，只有同一几何下解 4 帧以上才划算；该数据只有一个稳态流场，无法检验多帧分摊。检查点较粗（32、48、64）。Building the preconditioner costs 64 passes, more than one solve saves, so it pays off only over four or more frames with the same geometry; this dataset has a single steady flow, so amortisation over frames cannot be tested here. Checkpoints are coarse (32, 48, 64).
意义Why it matters
合成数据上的加速依赖先验足够平滑，不能直接照搬到真实数据。The speed-up seen on synthetic data relies on a smooth enough prior and does not carry over to real data as is.

到目前为止学到的What we have learned

  • 误差小，不等于省步数：一个用训练数据学出的修正把场误差降了约 30%，达到同等精度的步数却没有减少；决定步数的是观测残差。A smaller error does not mean fewer steps: a correction learned from training data cut the field error by about 30% yet saved no steps; the observation residual sets the count.
  • 算子调用少，不等于更快：一个经典初值少用了 72.7% 的调用，耗时却和对照几乎相同，内存还大得多。Fewer operator calls do not mean faster: one classical start used 72.7% fewer calls but took the same time and far more memory.
  • 训练损失下降，不等于重建更好：有几个小模型训练损失降了 13–27%，重建却没有改善。Lower training loss does not mean better reconstructions: several small models cut their training loss by 13–27% with no gain in the final result.
  • 先问“价值在哪个区间”，再设计方法：与其在强基线最强的地方硬拼，不如找到它代价最高的地方。Ask where the value lies before designing a method: compete where the strong baseline is expensive, not where it is strongest.
