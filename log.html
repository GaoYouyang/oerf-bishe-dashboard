论文 2 方向筛选：多数事先登记的方向是负结果，不确定性校准有稳健发现Paper 2 screening: most pre-registered directions are negative; uncertainty calibration gives robust findings

  • 以下方向都按事先写好的判据判为负结果：跨帧 Krylov 子空间回收（没达到预定的节省目标，且数值不稳定）；扩散模型先验采样（即使在训练轨迹上，场误差也约为匹配 Gaussian 的 3.2 倍）；贝叶斯最优视角设计（能正确给视角组合排序，但设计出的布局不优于均匀分布的环形布局）；考虑标定误差的重建（在实际的标定误差量级下，误差参数无法辨识，没有胜过对照）。这些只关闭了各自的具体方案，不代表整类方法不可行。Each of these was judged negative by criteria written in advance: cross-frame Krylov subspace recycling (missed its preset saving target and was numerically unstable); diffusion-prior posterior sampling (field error about 3.2× the matched Gaussian, even on a training trajectory); Bayesian optimal view design (it ranks view subsets correctly, but the designed layouts are no better than an evenly spread ring); calibration-aware reconstruction (at realistic calibration-error sizes the error parameters are not identifiable, and it did not beat the controls). These close the specific recipes, not whole method families.
  • 不确定性校准（同一真实装置的六段记录，用留出视角检验）：用证据最大化选超参数时，名义 90% 区间的实际覆盖率在六段记录上都只有 0.545–0.566，系统性过度自信。事先登记的重尾异方差误差模型在 4 段新记录中只有 1 段通过全部门槛，未获确认；但它的 90% 覆盖率在 4 段中都落在 0.85–0.95 之间，负对数预测密度也最低。Uncertainty calibration (six recordings from the same real rig, checked on a held-out view): choosing hyperparameters by evidence maximisation is systematically over-confident, with nominal 90% intervals covering only 0.545–0.566 in all six recordings. A pre-registered heavy-tailed, heteroscedastic error model passed every gate in only 1 of 4 new recordings and is not confirmed, but its 90% coverage stayed within 0.85–0.95 in all 4 and it had the lowest negative log predictive density.
  • 局限：六段记录来自同一台装置，相邻帧高度相关；覆盖率是在观测（视角）空间检验的，不等于三维场的覆盖率。论文 2 的方向尚未确定。Limits: the six recordings come from one rig and neighbouring frames are highly correlated; coverage is tested in observation (view) space, which is not the same as coverage of the 3D field. The direction for paper 2 is not settled.

2026-11-08

跨流动检验：调好的残差类停止准则不能迁移，冻结规则每帧都等价Cross-flow test: tuned residual-type stopping criteria do not transfer; the frozen rule matches every frame

  • 换一个新的流动族：公开的层流伴流火焰 CFD（扰动幅度 A0.3 和 A0.5，三种激励，无噪声和 1% 噪声），沿用合成的五相机几何，以精确后验为参考。每种停止准则各走一条因果链：每帧从它自己上一帧停下的结果出发，欠收敛会累积。三种常用残差类准则（相对残差、相对变化、Arioli 型能量估计）的阈值在真实装置的开发记录上调到零漏判；冻结规则不做任何调整。“等价”指四项指标都不超过精确后验的 1.01 倍。A new flow family: public CFD of a laminar coflow flame (forcing amplitudes A0.3 and A0.5, three forcing types, noise-free and 1% noise), on the reused synthetic five-camera geometry with the exact posterior as reference. Each stopping criterion runs its own causal chain, starting every frame from where it stopped on the previous one, so under-solving accumulates. The thresholds of three common residual-type criteria (relative residual, relative change, an Arioli-type energy estimate) were tuned to zero misses on development recordings from the real rig; the frozen rule was not adjusted. A frame matches when all four metrics are within 1.01× the exact posterior.
  • A0.3（数据已打开，事后分析）：冻结规则 354/354 帧等价；三种残差类准则都是 0/354；数据残差停滞准则 354/354，但迭代数是规则的 1.32 倍。A0.3 (opened data, post hoc): the frozen rule matched 354/354 frames; all three residual-type criteria 0/354; misfit stagnation 354/354 but with 1.32× the rule's iterations.
  • A0.5（数据下载前已登记判读方案，属前瞻检验）：冻结规则 354/354（最差比值 1.0014，门槛 1.01）；三种残差类准则仍是 0/354；数据残差停滞 354/354，迭代数为规则的 1.32 倍。在可靠的准则中规则最省。A0.5 (reading fixed before the data were downloaded, a prospective test): the frozen rule matched 354/354 (worst ratio 1.0014 against a 1.01 limit); the three residual-type criteria again 0/354; misfit stagnation 354/354 at 1.32× the rule's iterations. Among the reliable criteria the rule is the cheapest.
  • 局限：合成数据，几何沿用旧的五相机；在 A0.5 上规则的迭代数仍是事后用真值找到的最早停止点的约 1.6–2.5 倍。在同一真实装置上，调好的残差类准则在事先登记的另一段记录中只漏判 1 帧（一个异常亮的采集帧），并比规则少用约 36% 的调用。准确的说法是“在所选阈值下唯一零漏判的准则”，不是“其他准则都失败”。Limits: synthetic data on the reused five-camera geometry; on A0.5 the rule still uses about 1.6–2.5× the iterations of the earliest stop found afterwards with the ground truth. On the same real rig, the tuned residual-type criteria missed only 1 frame (an unusually bright acquisition frame) in another pre-registered recording and used about 36% fewer operator calls than the rule. The accurate claim is "the only criterion with zero misses at the chosen thresholds", not "every other criterion fails".

不看真值的停止规则通过真实数据封存检验A truth-free stopping rule passes a sealed real-data test

  • 11-04 的步数测量用真值判断何时停止，实际中不可用。为此设计了不看真值的停止规则：只有当估计的剩余数据误差相对当前残差足够小、且外推的场更新量相对后验场标准差 σf（只由先验和算子算出）足够小时才停。规则常数在合成数据的开发阶段冻结，之后没有改动。The 11-04 step counts stopped on ground truth, which is not available in practice. The new rule needs no truth: it stops only when the estimated remaining data error is small relative to the current misfit and the extrapolated field update is small relative to the posterior field standard deviation σf, which comes from the prior and operator alone. Its constants were frozen during synthetic development and not changed afterwards.
  • 数据：合作课题组的一套真实九视角多帧 BOS 记录（未公开数据，这里只报告汇总指标）。一个视角始终留出，不参与求解。“与参考等价”指：8 个求解视角的残差和留出视角的残差都不超过收敛到 1e-9 的参考解的 1.01 倍，且场与参考解的距离不超过 0.142 σf。Data: a real nine-view, multi-frame BOS recording from the collaborating group (unpublished; only summary metrics are reported here). One view is always held out and never used in the solve. A frame is reference-equivalent when the misfit on the 8 solved views and on the held-out view are both within 1.01× a reference solve converged to 1e-9, and the field lies within 0.142 σf of that reference.
  • 开发组 2000/2000 帧等价后，先登记并提交检验方案，再打开另外 3 组记录。3 组封存数据各 2000/2000 帧与参考等价，包含准备成本在内的算子调用是参考解的 0.439–0.443；中位成本为事后用真值找到的最早停止点的 1.83 倍。After 2000/2000 development frames matched, the test plan was registered and committed before three further recordings were opened. In each sealed recording 2000/2000 frames were reference-equivalent, using 0.439–0.443 of the reference's operator calls including set-up; the median cost was 1.83× the earliest stop found afterwards with the ground truth.
  • 局限：同一台装置、同一个先验。事先登记的对照显示，在开发组上用参考解标定的固定容差（3e-4）在封存组每组抽查的 400 帧上同样全部等价，并比规则少约 36% 的调用，所以规则的优势是不需要逐装置标定，而不是在已知装置上最省。正则化比最优值弱 4 个数量级的压力设置下规则未通过（此时参考解本身已过拟合）。“等价”是相对参考解而言，真实数据没有真值；成本按算子调用计。Limits: one rig and one prior. A pre-registered control shows that a fixed tolerance (3e-4) calibrated against reference solves on the development recording also matched all 400 frames checked in each sealed recording, with about 36% fewer calls than the rule; the rule's advantage is that it needs no per-rig calibration, not that it is cheapest on a known rig. In a stress setting with regularisation four orders of magnitude weaker than optimal the rule failed (the reference itself over-fits there). Equivalence is to the reference solve, since real data have no ground truth; cost is counted in operator calls.

换成白化原空间求解，步数再减半；真实数据上收益有条件The whitened primal halves the steps again; on real data the gain is conditional

  • 同一个 Gaussian 后验均值改在白化原空间里求解（ρ = μ + Lw，C = LLᵀ），从完全相同的初始场出发，步数约为对偶形式的一半。原空间 Nyström 秩 100 加前一帧只要 24–32 步（对偶形式秩 400 加前一帧为 32–48 步），准备成本也从 400 次降到 150 次算子调用。Solving the same Gaussian posterior mean in the whitened primal (ρ = μ + Lw, C = LLᵀ) takes about half the steps of the dual form from exactly the same starting field. Primal rank-100 Nyström with the previous frame needs 24–32 steps (dual rank 400 with the previous frame: 32–48), and set-up drops from 400 to 150 operator calls.
  • 剩下的步数主要花在观测残差上，每次运行都是它最后达标：场和梯度约 8–12 步就达标，观测残差要 24–32 步。用其他轨迹学出的线性修正把场误差降了约 30%，步数却没有减少；外推和子空间最优投影也几乎不省。学习型初值必须降低观测残差才有用。The remaining steps go mostly into the observation residual, the last metric to match in every run: the field and gradients match after about 8–12 steps, the observation residual after 24–32. A linear correction learned from other trajectories cut the field error by about 30% but saved no steps, and extrapolation or the best start in a span of past solutions saved almost none. A learned start helps only if it lowers the observation residual.
  • 宾州州立大学公开的真实数据（1063 万条射线，32³ 网格）：不加平滑先验时 Nyström 没有净收益，都要 64 次投影；加高斯平滑先验后，秩 64 在 48 次达标（零初值 64 次），但准备成本要在同一几何下分摊到 4 帧以上才划算。Penn State public real data (10.6 million rays, 32³ grid): without a smoothing prior Nyström gives no net gain (64 passes either way); with a Gaussian smoothness prior rank 64 meets the criterion at 48 passes against 64 from zero, but its set-up pays off only over four or more frames with the same geometry.
  • 局限：合成数据部分是无噪声数据、五相机，只测了 9 条轨迹中 3 条的 9–12 个开发帧，其余 6 条留作确认集，尚未评估；停止点用真值判断，是诊断性测量，不是实际可用的停止规则。真实数据只有一个稳态流场。Limits: the synthetic tests use noise-free data with five cameras and cover 9–12 development frames from 3 of the 9 trajectories, with the other 6 kept as a confirmation set and not yet evaluated; stopping is judged against ground truth, so these are diagnostics, not a practical stopping rule. The real dataset has a single steady flow.
2026-11-04

成本前沿：经典加速把迭代成本压得很低Cost frontier: classical acceleration makes the iterative solve cheap

  • 随机 Nyström 预条件只用几何、不用训练数据，把达到精确 Gaussian 精度所需的步数从 384 降到 64（秩 400）和 24（秩 1600）；再用前一帧的结果做初值，秩 400 时降到 48。A randomized Nyström preconditioner, built from the geometry alone, cut the steps needed to match the exact Gaussian from 384 to 64 (rank 400) and 24 (rank 1600); starting from the previous frame brought rank 400 down to 48.
  • 所需步数约随测量数的平方根增长；1% 噪声下多约 3 倍，而预条件在那里的加速也更大（秩 400 和 1600 分别快 6 倍和 16 倍）。The step count grows roughly with the square root of the measurement count; at 1% noise it triples, and the preconditioner helps more there too (6× at rank 400, 16× at rank 1600).
  • 用其他轨迹的解构造初值子空间完全无效；用前几帧做线性或二次外推，或用其他轨迹的数据构造预条件，也没有比直接用前一帧更好。A starting subspace built from other trajectories' solutions did not help at all, and neither linear or quadratic extrapolation from earlier frames nor a preconditioner built from other trajectories' data did better than the previous frame.
  • 余量测量：初值误差若能降到前一帧的 1/3，步数还能再降约 3 倍；降到 1/10，只需 2–8 步。这给学习型初值定下了具体的精度目标。Headroom: a start three times closer than the previous frame would cut the steps about 3× further; ten times closer needs only 2–8 steps. This gives a learned start a concrete accuracy target.
2026-11-03

方向转折：学习型初值的价值在大规模测量Turning point: a learned start pays off at large measurement counts

  • 核对成本账：固定相机时，Gaussian 对照只需一次矩阵分解，之后每帧几乎不花成本。Cost audit: with fixed cameras the Gaussian baseline needs one matrix factorisation, after which each frame is almost free.
  • 迭代求解在一帧上要约 384 步才能与精确 Gaussian 同等精度；真实相机的测量规模下只能迭代。The iterative solve needed about 384 steps on one frame to match the exact Gaussian; at real camera scale iteration is the only option.
  • 一个利用前一帧重建结果的时序模型完成训练，但沿 101 帧反向传播时梯度从约 80 增长到 1e234，最终误差是 Gaussian 的约 4.9 倍，还不如它自己的起点。判定为该训练方案失败；今后训练改为按预设规则提前止损。A temporal model that reuses the previous frame finished training, but back-propagating through 101 frames was unstable (gradients grew from ~80 to 1e234) and its final error was ~4.9× the Gaussian, worse than its own starting point. The recipe is recorded as failed; future training stops early under pre-set futility rules.
  • 下一步：测量所需步数随测量数的变化，并找到一套公开的真实 BOS 层析数据用于验证。Next: measure how the required steps scale with the number of measurements, and use a public real BOS tomography dataset for validation.

2026-10 · 第 4–5 周weeks 4–5

原生网格上的大量尝试没有超过 GaussianMany native-grid designs did not beat the Gaussian

  • 几十种初值设计在训练轨迹上就达不到 Gaussian 精度；以 Gaussian 为底的学习修正只改进约 0.01%。Dozens of designs fell short of the Gaussian even on training trajectories; a learned correction on top of it improved by only ~0.01%.
