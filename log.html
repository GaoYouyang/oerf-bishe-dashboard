进展日志Progress Log

按时间倒序记录阶段性进展。逐项实验的详细记录保留在归档中。Milestones in reverse chronological order. Detailed per-experiment records are kept in the archive.

2026-11-04

换成白化原空间求解，步数再减半；真实数据上收益有条件The whitened primal halves the steps again; on real data the gain is conditional

  • 同一个 Gaussian 后验均值改在白化原空间里求解（ρ = μ + Lw，C = LLᵀ），从完全相同的初始场出发，步数约为对偶形式的一半。原空间 Nyström 秩 100 加前一帧只要 24–32 步（对偶形式秩 400 加前一帧为 32–48 步），准备成本也从 400 次降到 150 次算子调用。Solving the same Gaussian posterior mean in the whitened primal (ρ = μ + Lw, C = LLᵀ) takes about half the steps of the dual form from exactly the same starting field. Primal rank-100 Nyström with the previous frame needs 24–32 steps (dual rank 400 with the previous frame: 32–48), and set-up drops from 400 to 150 operator calls.
  • 剩下的步数主要花在观测残差上：场和梯度约 8–12 步就达标，观测残差要 24–32 步。用其他轨迹学出的线性修正把场误差降了约 30%，步数却没有减少；外推和子空间最优投影也几乎不省。学习型初值必须降低观测残差才有用。The remaining steps go mostly into the observation residual: the field and gradients match after about 8–12 steps, the observation residual after 24–32. A linear correction learned from other trajectories cut the field error by about 30% but saved no steps, and extrapolation or the best start in a span of past solutions saved almost none. A learned start helps only if it lowers the observation residual.
  • 宾州州立大学公开的真实数据（1063 万条射线，32³ 网格）：不加平滑先验时 Nyström 没有净收益，都要 64 次投影；加高斯平滑先验后，秩 64 在 48 次达标（零初值 64 次），但准备成本要在同一几何下分摊到 4 帧以上才划算。Penn State public real data (10.6 million rays, 32³ grid): without a smoothing prior Nyström gives no net gain (64 passes either way); with a Gaussian smoothness prior rank 64 meets the criterion at 48 passes against 64 from zero, but its set-up pays off only over four or more frames with the same geometry.
  • 局限：合成数据部分只测了 9–12 帧，停止点用真值判断，是诊断性测量；真实数据只有一个稳态流场。Limits: the synthetic tests cover 9–12 frames and stop on ground truth, so they are diagnostics; the real dataset has a single steady flow.
2026-11-04

成本前沿：经典加速把迭代成本压得很低Cost frontier: classical acceleration makes the iterative solve cheap

  • 随机 Nyström 预条件只用几何、不用训练数据，把达到精确 Gaussian 精度所需的步数从 384 降到 64（秩 400）和 24（秩 1600）；再用前一帧的结果做初值，秩 400 时降到 48。A randomized Nyström preconditioner, built from the geometry alone, cut the steps needed to match the exact Gaussian from 384 to 64 (rank 400) and 24 (rank 1600); starting from the previous frame brought rank 400 down to 48.
  • 所需步数约随测量数的平方根增长；1% 噪声下多约 3 倍，而预条件在那里的加速也更大（秩 400 和 1600 分别快 6 倍和 16 倍）。The step count grows roughly with the square root of the measurement count; at 1% noise it triples, and the preconditioner helps more there too (6× at rank 400, 16× at rank 1600).
  • 用其他轨迹的解构造初值子空间完全无效；用前几帧做线性或二次外推，或用其他轨迹的数据构造预条件，也没有比直接用前一帧更好。A starting subspace built from other trajectories' solutions did not help at all, and neither linear or quadratic extrapolation from earlier frames nor a preconditioner built from other trajectories' data did better than the previous frame.
  • 余量测量：初值误差若能降到前一帧的 1/3，步数还能再降约 3 倍；降到 1/10，只需 2–8 步。这给学习型初值定下了具体的精度目标。Headroom: a start three times closer than the previous frame would cut the steps about 3× further; ten times closer needs only 2–8 steps. This gives a learned start a concrete accuracy target.
2026-11-03

方向转折：学习型初值的价值在大规模测量Turning point: a learned start pays off at large measurement counts

  • 核对成本账：固定相机时，Gaussian 对照只需一次矩阵分解，之后每帧几乎不花成本。Cost audit: with fixed cameras the Gaussian baseline needs one matrix factorisation, after which each frame is almost free.
  • 迭代求解在一帧上要约 384 步才能与精确 Gaussian 同等精度；真实相机的测量规模下只能迭代。The iterative solve needed about 384 steps on one frame to match the exact Gaussian; at real camera scale iteration is the only option.
  • 一个利用前一帧重建结果的时序模型完成训练，但沿 101 帧反向传播时梯度从约 80 增长到 1e234，最终误差是 Gaussian 的约 4.9 倍，还不如它自己的起点。判定为该训练方案失败；今后训练改为按预设规则提前止损。A temporal model that reuses the previous frame finished training, but back-propagating through 101 frames was unstable (gradients grew from ~80 to 1e234) and its final error was ~4.9× the Gaussian, worse than its own starting point. The recipe is recorded as failed; future training stops early under pre-set futility rules.
  • 下一步：测量所需步数随测量数的变化，并找到一套公开的真实 BOS 层析数据用于验证。Next: measure how the required steps scale with the number of measurements, and use a public real BOS tomography dataset for validation.
2026-10-29

原生网格的强对照定型Strong baseline settled on the native grid

  • 改进后的 Gaussian 对照在 9/9 条训练轨迹上满足全部精度上限。The improved Gaussian baseline meets every limit on 9/9 training trajectories.
  • 利用水平旋转与镜像对称的先验，把最难轨迹的梯度误差从 0.855 降到 0.815，但留出检验仍是 7/9。A rotation/mirror symmetry prior reduced the hardest trajectory's gradient error from 0.855 to 0.815, but held-out tests still pass 7/9.
2026-10 · 第 4–5 周weeks 4–5

原生网格上的大量尝试没有超过 GaussianMany native-grid designs did not beat the Gaussian

  • 几十种初值设计在训练轨迹上就达不到 Gaussian 精度；以 Gaussian 为底的学习修正只改进约 0.01%。Dozens of designs fell short of the Gaussian even on training trajectories; a learned correction on top of it improved by only ~0.01%.
  • 结论：问题不在泛化或算力，而在当前规模下 Gaussian 已接近可达精度。Lesson: the issue is not generalisation or compute; at this scale the Gaussian is near the achievable accuracy.
2026-10 · 第 2–3 周weeks 2–3

粗网格上的学习先验：有收益，也有代价Coarse-grid learned prior: gains with trade-offs

  • 1010 个留出帧全部达到参考精度，场与梯度误差降低 9.25%；两条轨迹的观测误差超限。All 1,010 held-out frames matched the reference with 9.25% lower field/gradient error; two trajectories exceeded the observation limit.
  • 不重训迁移到 7/9/12 相机、少一台相机和 1% 噪声；首次完整运行快约 17–18%，但内存略高。Transferred without retraining to 7/9/12 cameras, a removed camera and 1% noise; a full first run was ~17–18% faster but used slightly more memory.
  • 10 月 5 日确定 Dr Kelvin Ooi 为校内导师，与上海交大蔡老师课题组继续合作。5 Oct: Dr Kelvin Ooi confirmed as internal supervisor; collaboration with Prof. Cai's group at SJTU continues.
