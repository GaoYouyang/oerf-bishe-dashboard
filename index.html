多相机 BOS 层析的学习型初值Learning-Based Warm Starts for Multi-View BOS Tomography

多台相机透过火焰拍摄背景图案的微小位移，可以反推出火焰的三维密度场。这个反问题很难，求解器要反复计算很多次。我研究的是：用学习得到的初值，能否在不降低精度的前提下，减少物理求解器的计算量。 Several cameras record how a flame bends the view of a patterned background; from these small displacements we reconstruct the flame's 3D density. The inverse problem is hard and solvers iterate many times. This project asks whether a learned initial guess can cut the solver's cost without losing accuracy.

目前的核心发现Key findings so far

最新（2026-11-04）Latest (2026-11-04)

迭代求解的经典门槛已经很低：只用相机几何的随机 Nyström 预条件加前一帧暖启动，把达到 Gaussian 精度所需的步数从约 384 降到 48；把同一问题改在白化原空间里求解，再降到 24–32 步。剩下的步数主要花在让观测残差达标上，所以学习型初值必须降低观测残差，单纯把场预测得更准并不省步数。详见结果页。The classical bar for the iterative solve is already low: a randomized Nyström preconditioner built from the camera geometry alone, started from the previous frame, cuts the steps needed to match the Gaussian from about 384 to 48, and solving the same problem in the whitened primal brings it to 24–32. The remaining steps are spent mostly on the observation residual, so a learned start has to lower that residual; predicting the field more accurately alone does not save steps. See Results.

前向与伴随算子由两套独立实现交叉检验，差异约 1e-12。原生网格 200×80×80，9 条轨迹共 909 帧。Forward and adjoint operators are cross-checked by two independent implementations (≈1e-12 agreement) on a 200×80×80 grid, 9 trajectories, 909 frames.

已核实verified

统计对照非常强The statistical baseline is very strong

Gaussian 条件均值在 9/9 条训练轨迹上达标；在它之上再加学习修正，联合误差只降低约 0.01%。The Gaussian posterior mean meets every limit on 9/9 training trajectories; learned corrections on top of it reduce the joint error by only ~0.01%.

有收益，有代价gain with trade-offs

粗网格上学习先验有效Learned prior helps on a coarse grid

在 1010 个留出帧上，场与梯度误差降低 9.25%，不重训即可迁移到 7/9/12 相机和含噪数据；但有两条轨迹的观测误差超限，内存也更高。On 1,010 held-out frames, field/gradient error fell by 9.25% and the model transferred to 7/9/12 cameras and noise without retraining, but two trajectories exceeded the observation limit and memory was higher.

方向转折turning point

学习的价值在大规模测量Learning pays off at large measurement counts

在约 1 万个测量点时，预先分解的 Gaussian 每帧几乎不花成本；迭代求解则要约 384 步才达到同等精度。真实相机有上千万像素，那时只能迭代，这才是学习型初值的用武之地。At ~10⁴ measurements the pre-factorised Gaussian is almost free per frame, while the iterative version needs ~384 steps to match it. Real cameras give ~10⁷ measurements, where only iteration is possible — that is where a learned start can help.
